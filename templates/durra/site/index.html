The setting room

Light, set by hand.

Fourteen goldsmiths and setters, two microscopes, and no machine that touches a stone.

How a piece is made

01

01

Drawing

Every piece starts as a gouache drawing at full size, painted until the proportions feel right on the body.

02

02

Setting

Setters raise tiny beads of gold to hold each stone, working under a microscope.

03

03

Finishing

Polishing, a final check under the loupe, and a hallmark struck in Dubai.
