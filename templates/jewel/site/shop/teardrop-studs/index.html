Teardrop Studs

A hand-polished 18k gold chain bracelet with a signature lobster clasp.

Description

The Teardrop Studs capture the softness of first light in a pair of perfectly matched pear-cut morganites. Each 0.7 carat stone is hand-selected for its signature peach-pink blush and bezel-set in 14-karat rose gold for a smooth, snag-free profile that sits flush against the earlobe. The rounded edges of the bezel are polished to a liquid shine, creating a warm glow that complements every skin tone. Secured with hypoallergenic butterfly backs and threaded posts for comfortable all-day wear. Sold as a pair and delivered in a Jewel© presentation box lined with ivory silk — the quiet luxury of an everyday piece that feels like a quiet indulgence.

Delivery

All orders over $150 ship free worldwide. Each piece is carefully packaged in our signature jewelry box and dispatched within 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days, with express options available for 2-3 day shipping. Every shipment is fully insured and tracked, requiring a signature upon arrival to ensure your jewelry reaches you safely.

Return

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unworn pieces. Simply contact our team to receive a prepaid return label, and you'll receive a full refund within 5-7 business days. Exchanges are always complimentary. Every piece comes with a lifetime craftsmanship guarantee—if you experience any manufacturing defects, we'll repair or replace it free of charge.
